ROOK IN 30 SECONDS

It reasons with competing worlds.

1
Observe

Start with evidence that multiple explanations may fit.

2
Compete

Keep several executable explanations alive instead of forcing one answer.

3
Test

Find the observation or intervention where those explanations disagree most.

4
Update

Use the result to change belief, reject a theory, or invent a better one.

The built-in worlds are controlled synthetic environments so hidden truth is known to the evaluator. “Your data” can analyze a numeric CSV with a frozen holdout, but observational data alone still does not establish causality.
